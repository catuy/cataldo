Small Business Development Group (SBDG) is a publicly traded U.S. holding company focused on acquiring and scaling small to medium-sized businesses across North America. With operations spanning multiple states and international offices, SBDG emphasizes strategic alignment, strong business fundamentals, and long-term value creation.

Paipo partnered with SBDG to design and develop a custom internal platform supporting their operational growth and acquisition pipeline. We provided strategic input to define product requirements and ensure alignment with business goals, while building a tailored solution that fit their internal processes and stakeholder workflows.

Our work combined hands-on development with product direction to enable faster decision-making and improved operational visibility across SBDG's portfolio companies.

- 1 Project Lead
